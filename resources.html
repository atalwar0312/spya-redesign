Common questions

Before you begin.

How do I register?

Start with the sport commissioner to confirm the current registration link and your child’s eligibility.

Registration guidance
Are tryouts required?

Some programs use tryouts or evaluations. Ask about the process for your child’s sport and division.

What equipment will we need?

Confirm the program’s equipment list before buying anything. Some programs provide or loan equipment.

Who can explain fees and refunds?

Your sport commissioner can confirm the applicable fees and refund terms before you register.
