Common Questions

Before You Begin

Are Tryouts Required?

Selection varies by sport and division. Some teams hold tryouts; others use evaluations to balance teams. Confirm the process for your child’s program.

What Equipment Will We Need?

Check your program’s equipment list before buying anything. Some programs provide or loan equipment; availability and deposits vary.

Where Will We Play?

Locations vary by sport and team. Use your team’s communications for practice locations and last-minute changes.

How Do Fees and Refunds Work?

Review the terms for your sport before registering. Travel programs may have additional costs, and refund exceptions differ by program. Ask the commissioner about your circumstances.

Can I Get Help With Online Registration?

Contact your sport commissioner if you cannot complete the online process. Each program page lists the appropriate email address.
