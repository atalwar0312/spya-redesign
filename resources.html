How do I register?

Use the official SPYA registration page after confirming your child's eligibility and the program's availability. Registration is handled through TeamSnap.

See registration guidance
Can I get a refund?

Published sport pages and bylaws describe different circumstances and exceptions. Confirm the current refund policy with the commissioner before paying.

Does my child need to attend tryouts?

It depends on the program. Some competitive teams require tryouts. Field Hockey describes evaluations to balance teams. Check your sport page and ask the commissioner.

What equipment should we buy?

Wait until you have the current program equipment list. Some programs supply or loan equipment; others require separate purchases.

Where will practices take place?

Locations vary by sport and team. Published examples include Grange Park and local schools. Confirm your assigned location and schedule before attending.
