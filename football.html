Program Overview

Flag Football

The introductory flag division within SPYA Football is separate from NFL Flag. Confirm the correct program before choosing a form.

Tackle Football

Junior Pee Wee, Pee Wee, Junior Varsity, and Varsity divisions combine football skills, safety, teamwork, and sportsmanship. Pee Wee through Varsity compete in a competitive league.

2026 Age and Weight Rules

Eligibility uses January 1 age cutoffs. Junior Pee Wee players may not have turned 8 before January 1; Pee Wee players may not have turned 9; Junior Varsity players may not have turned 11; Varsity players may not have turned 13. Anyone turning 14 during 2026 is ineligible. Confirm placement with the commissioner.

2026 Weight Limits

Published skill-player/lineman limits are: Junior Pee Wee, 80/140 lb; Pee Wee, 100/140 lb; Junior Varsity, 120/140 lb; Varsity, 140/160 lb. There is no older/lighter Junior Varsity option.

2026 Pee Wee Exception

A returning 2025 player born August 1–December 31, 2016 may choose Pee Wee for the same organization in 2026. This affects future Junior Varsity eligibility, and double rostering at Junior Varsity is not permitted under this exception. Discuss the complete rules with the commissioner.
