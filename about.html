About

Local Teams. Shared Purpose.

A nonprofit, volunteer-run youth sports association in Allentown, Pennsylvania.

Our Mission

Our mission is to help neighborhood youth develop teamwork, sportsmanship, character, and community spirit through the challenge and enjoyment of team sports.

We want young people to enjoy participating and feel proud to wear an SPYA uniform.
