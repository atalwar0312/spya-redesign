About & contact

Our community. Their future.

A volunteer-run youth sports association serving the Parkland community in and around Allentown, Pennsylvania.

What brings us together

A love of sport.
A sense of belonging.

SPYA helps neighborhood youth develop teamwork, sportsmanship, character and community spirit through team sports.

Our purpose is simple: help children enjoy participating and feel proud to be part of their team.

Find the right person.

For eligibility, schedules, equipment or registration, start with the relevant sport commissioner.
