About SPYA

Local teams. Shared purpose.

Helping neighborhood youth learn, participate and grow through team sports.

Our mission

South Parkland Youth Association is a nonprofit, volunteer-run organization based in Allentown, Pennsylvania.

Through team sports, SPYA helps young people develop teamwork, sportsmanship, character and community spirit.
