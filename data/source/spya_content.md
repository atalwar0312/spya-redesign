SPYA - Who Are We?
South Parkland Youth Association (SPYA) is a non-profit organization which provides sports programs for boys and girls, ages 4 to 18. The sports currently offered are Baseball, Basketball, Cheerleading, Field Hockey, Football, Lacrosse, Soccer, and Softball.
When our Sport Programs are offered:
Baseball
Spring (baseball, t-ball and blastball) and Fall (baseball only) sport, with opportunities in both travel and intramural programs. Registrations held in January and May of each year. Spring teams are formed in late March; practices start early April, with games being held from May to June. Fall teams are formed in June with games from August through October.
Basketball
Winter sport, with opportunities in both travel and intramural programs. Registration held in September of each year. Teams are formed in mid-November, and games are played from January to March.
Cheerleading
Fall sport only, with opportunities in both intramural and competition programs. Registration held in May of each year.
Field Hockey
Fall sport for Tiny's (K-2) only.  However, we do offer both a fall and spring program for girls in grades 3/4 and 5/6.  See our field hockey web page for more details.
Football
Fall sport (please see our Football page on this site for more info on the different age and weight category team breakdowns). Registration held in May of each year.
Lacrosse
Spring program with additional training offered throughout the year.  Please contact the Commissioner for additional details.
Softball
Spring softball, with opportunities in both intramural and travel programs. Registration held in January of each year.  Fall softball is also offered with registration held in May of each year.
Soccer
Fall and Spring sport, with opportunities in both intramural and travel programs. Note: Travel is a 2 season commitment, with tryouts in June of each year. Fall registration is in May, and Spring is in January.
Registrations
All registrations are done via our website and payment is made via PayPal.
January (Baseball, Softball, Spring Soccer, Field Hockey, Lacrosse)
May (Fall Baseball, Cheerleading, Football, Fall Soccer, Fall Softball, Field Hockey)
September (Basketball)
Electronic flyers are distributed to each Parkland School District elementary and middle schools, the month prior to each registration, with the specific dates, times, location, and other sport-specific info. We also advertise the registration dates on our webpage. There are a few occasions where an additional registration is added for a specific sport needs; flyers will be distributed and the website updated for these dates as well.
FAQ
What if I have a question regarding a specific sport?
Expand
Each sport has a chairperson or "commissioner", who is in charge of overseeing all aspects of that sport. These commissioners are listed under the Board Members section of this website, along with their email addresses. Please feel free to send them any questions.
How do we find out about travel team tryouts?
Expand
Dates and times of tryouts are usually mentioned on the registration flyers. This info is also made available as a handout at the in-person registration, and as a printout for online registration. You can also email the respective commissioner to get any info you need.
What if I would like to coach?
Expand
SPYA is always looking for volunteers interested in helping the kids, and coaching is a very important aspect of that. You can indicate an interest in either Head Coach or Asst. Coach on your registration form, under Volunteering. If you have any questions beforehand, please feel free to contact a commissioner. SPYA tries to as many in-house clinics and licensing courses as possible, and our commissioners are aware of a number of outside opportunities if you would like to increase your knowledge of any sport. Fees for these are refundable by SPYA.

Welcome to the South Parkland Baseball website! Our mission is to provide a safe, supportive, and skill-focused environment where young athletes can develop not only as players but also as individuals. With a commitment to teamwork, sportsmanship, and character-building, we strive to empower our players with the tools they need to succeed both on and off the field. Whether they're taking their first steps onto the diamond or preparing to transition to higher levels of play, we're here to guide them every step of the way. Join us in this exciting journey of learning, growth, and a shared love for the game!
Here is some important information:
Capacity Limits
- Once they are met, a waitlist will be created by contacting the Baseball Commissioner at baseball@spya.org. Once enough players are on the waitlist to create a team, and a head coach has been identified, you will be contacted to complete your registration.
Registration Closing Date
- Due to tryout schedules and the formation of teams, the Registration Closing Date is final and cannot be changed.
Tryouts
- In the Spring, Biddy and Midget will have tryouts in early March, while all other levels will be held in late March once the high school roster has been set.  In the Fall, all divisions will tryout in late July. Players must attend independent evaluated tryouts to be eligible for PYL AL, LVL, or Connie Mack team selection and are strongly encourage to attend all try out sessions.  Players not selected will be made available for the PYL NL teams, who will build their rosters via a draft.  Players registered will be contacted directly with details of their respective tryout.  There is no additional cost for tryouts at any level.
Financial Aid
- SPYA has a financial aid application and process for families in need.  It is completed in the strictest confidence.  You may request further information at treasurer@spya.org.
Refunds
- SPYA has a strict no refund policy, if there is a place for your child to play there will be no refund.
Tournament Players
- We encourage you to play within South Parkland to supplement your tournament season.  To be eligible to play within SP, you must try out for the LVL/PYL AL or Jr. Connie Mack team at your age level.  If you do not make one of these teams you are not eligible for a PYL NL team.  A partial refund will be determined directly by the Baseball Commissioner, with a max refund of 2/3 of the registration fee.  You will need to provide proof of being rostered for the current season of your tournament team along with documented attendance at SP tryouts.
Residency Requirements
- LVL, Connie Mack, and Legion teams have residency requirements based on your home address.  If you do not live within South Parkland territory, you must have been cut or released from your home team to be eligible for South Parkland.  For further information, please contact the Baseball Commissioner at baseball@spya.org

Evaluations
SPYA Field Hockey believes firmly in forming evenly skilled teams. We do not stack teams with the best players, nor do we form teams with players who are only new to the game or need to be developed. As such, every child participating in the fall season must undergo a mandatory evaluation.
This is NOT a tryout
. This is a series of drills to understand where the kids rank on their skill in order to form even teams. Information on when to sign up for evaluations will be made available in July 2026 for our upcoming fall season.

Philosophy
SPYA FH is a recreational league. It is a place for kids to learn the game of field hockey, develop their skill, make friendships, all while competing in a safe and fun environment. We participate each fall in the Lehigh Valley Youth Field Hockey League, where games are held at each grade level. There are no tournaments, no rankings and no championships. More information can be found on our league and who participates at

## T-Ball, Prep, Minors – South Parkland Youth
Source: https://spya.org/baseball/t-ball-prep-minors/

In-House Programs
Questions? : Contact
baseball@spya.org
Our baseball in-house programs cater to the youngest players, providing an ideal platform for them to embark on their baseball journey. Our primary focus lies in cultivating a love for the game and fostering player development. Our dedicated coaches create a nurturing environment where each child's growth is celebrated, both on and off the field.
T-Ball
Ages: 4 and 5 (Cannot be 6 before May 1, 2025)
Practice: One or two days a week
Location: Cetronia Elementary
Program Description
: A program recommended for 4- and 5-year-olds to teach the fundamentals of hitting and fielding. All batters will hit the ball from a batting tee which is adjusted to height that allows for a level swing. All players present for the game bat in order each inning as well as play a defensive position in each of the four innings. Towards the end of the season, the basics of coach pitch will be introduced into one inning of each game if agreed upon by both teams. Adult coaches are stationed on the field to provide instruction. The primary goal is to begin to teach young players the fundamentals of baseball in a supportive team setting while preparing the players for the next level (Coach Pitch). The T- Ball level will use 50 foot bases. Players should have their own glove for T-Ball. Purchasing of your own baseball bat is optional as they are provided by the league.
Coach Pitch
Ages: 6 and 7  (Cannot be 8 before May 1, 2025)
Practice and Games : Three days a week
Location: Lone Lane Park
Program Description
: The Coach Pitch League is a coach pitch league designed primarily for 6–7-year-olds. The Prep League is designed to teach the fundamentals of hitting and fielding. All players present for the game bat in order each inning as well as play a defensive position in each inning. Towards the end of the season, the basics of kid pitch will be introduced into one inning of each game if agreed upon by both teams. Adult coaches are stationed on the field to provide instruction. The Coach Pitch League continues teaching the fundamentals of the game of baseball in a safe, supportive team environment while preparing the players for the next level (Grasshopper). Players should have their own glove. Purchasing of your own baseball bat is optional as they are provided by the league.
Grasshopper
Age: 7 and 8 (Cannot be 9 before May 1, 2025)
Practice and Games : Three days a week with rotating days and times
Location: Parkway Manor Elementary
Program Description
: The Grasshopper League is a coach pitch / kid pitch combo league designed primarily for 7- & 8-year-olds to prepare for advancing to full kid pitch in the fall. The league is designed to expand upon the fundamentals of hitting and fielding learned at previous levels. All players present for the game bat in order each inning as well as play a defensive position each inning. This is a primary player pitch with a coach coming in under league specific rules. This allows the players to begin to get a feel for pitching they may want to do at the next level of play. Batters cannot walk against kid pitching, if a ball 4 count happens, a coach will step in to finish the at-bat. The Minors level continues teaching the fundamentals of the game of baseball in a safe, supportive team environment. Grasshopper will use 60 foot bases. Players will need to have their own glove and bat.

## Parkland Youth League – South Parkland Youth
Source: https://spya.org/baseball/parkland-youth-league/

Parkland Youth League (PYL)
PYL Website
PYL Rules and Equipment Information
An alliance of neighboring youth organizations has united to create an association offering 9-18 year-olds an avenue for baseball. With a focus on fundamentals, enjoyment, and healthy competition, teams engage in 10-12 games per season followed by a playoff tournament, instilling both skill development and sportsmanship.
The setup comprises two divisions: NL (developmental) and AL (competitive). Securing a spot on an AL team mandates participation in tryouts, wherein coaches and evaluators collaboratively select the AL division teams. Those not chosen for AL will be assigned to an NL team.
Spring 2023 PYL Champions and Runner-Ups
Biddy Division
Coordinator: Aaron Markowitz
Email: agmark22@gmail.com
Ages: 9 and 10
Fee: $150
Biddy level baseball mirrors the high school game's initial essence, featuring umpires and a heightened competitive spirit. The absence of coaches on the field marks a transition towards independence. Teams in this tier engage in 2-3 weekly practices and participate in approximately 2 games per week during the season.
Midget Division
Coordinator: Jon Knoblach
Email: jonknoblach@gmail.com
Ages: 11 and 12
Fee: $150
The Midget level marks the removal of prior restrictions, granting players the freedom to lead and steal bases, escalating the competitive intensity. During pre-season, teams practice around 3 times weekly, and during the season, they engage in approximately 2 games a week alongside integrated practices.
Knee-Hi Division
Coordinator: Don Friday
Email: baseball@spya.org
Ages: 13, 14, and 15
Fee: $150
Knee-Hi level players are poised at the final juncture before High School Baseball. With base lengths extending to 80 feet (NL) and 90 feet (AL), the game adopts a high school baseball ambiance, characterized by reduced run counts and fewer errors. Similar to the Midget level, teams practice around 3 times weekly, and play in approximately 2 games once the season begins.
Majors Division
Coordinator: Don Friday
Email: baseball@spya.org
Ages: 16, 17, and 18
Fee: $150
The Majors division caters to our senior athletes, marking the culmination of their youth baseball journey. Comprising high school players, this division adheres to authentic MLB field dimensions and rules. Weekly practices occur 2-3 times, followed by around 2 games each week once the season commences. To ensure harmony with graduation ceremonies, the Spring season kicks off a bit earlier compared to other divisions, allowing a seamless conclusion to the season.
