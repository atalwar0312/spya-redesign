About the program

T-Ball

An introduction to hitting and fielding with a batting tee and on-field coaching.

Coach Pitch

Coach-pitched batting and instruction to help young players develop their skills.

Grasshopper

A transition toward player pitching, supported by coaches under league rules.

League baseball

Additional pathways include Parkland Youth League, Lehigh Valley League, Connie Mack and Senior Legion.
