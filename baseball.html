Explore the program

Room to grow.

T-Ball

An introduction to hitting and fielding with a batting tee and on-field coaching. The published program meets one or two days per week at Cetronia Elementary. Bring a glove; league bats are available.

Coach Pitch

Coach-pitched batting and supportive instruction. The published program lists three days per week at Lone Lane Park. Bring a glove; league bats are available.

Grasshopper

A transition toward player pitching, with coaches supporting play under league rules. Published sessions take place three days per week at Parkway Manor Elementary. A glove and bat are required.

League pathways

Parkland Youth League offers developmental and competitive divisions. Lehigh Valley League, Connie Mack and Senior Legion have specific selection and eligibility requirements.

Before you registerIntroductory age cutoffs in the source still refer to 2025. Confirm the current division, schedule and equipment list before enrolling.
