About the program

Youth softball

The program typically fields 8U, 10U and 12U teams. A 14U team depends on interest.

Playing location

Practices and home games are held at Springhouse Middle School, 1200 Springhouse Road, Allentown.

For current eligibility, fees and schedules, contact the program commissioner.
