Good to know

All SPYA registrations are online through TeamSnap. Forms accept credit card and bank account payments. The association may close registration early based on facilities, roster capacity, or coach availability.

SPYA may charge additional fees for travel teams. Contact the relevant sport commissioner if you cannot register online.
