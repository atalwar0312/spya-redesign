Program Overview

Recreational 5v5

A recreational flag football program for a range of skill levels, with fall, winter, and spring seasons. The program also describes skills training between fall and winter.

Age-Group Divisions

Divisions are 7U, 9U, 11U, 13U, and 15U. The upper-age cutoff dates are July 1 for fall, December 1 for winter, and April 1 for spring. Confirm eligibility for the intended season.

Game Format

The published program describes 8–10 games per season plus at least one playoff game. Exact schedules are supplied by the program.

Choose the Correct Program

NFL Flag is separate from the flag division within SPYA Football. The two programs have different registration forms.

Fall 2026 Season

The published season runs August 22–October 25, 2026. Use team communications for individual game times and changes.
