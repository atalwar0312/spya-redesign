Program Overview

Cheerleading

Cheer supports team spirit and participation. The published 2026 program covers kindergarten through eighth grade.

2026 Team Groups

Flags: kindergarten; Junior Pee Wee: first grade; Pee Wee: second and third grades; Junior Varsity: fourth and fifth grades; Varsity: sixth through eighth grades. Groups are subject to change based on participation.

Season Equipment

The 2026 program says cheerleaders will receive the equipment they need for the season. Confirm fittings, distribution, and any personal items with the program.
