Sports

Lacrosse

Introductory and team lacrosse programs.

Program Overview

Mini-Scoopers

An introduction to lacrosse through play, with sticks and soft balls provided. Ask about the next session, current ages, and capacity.

Boys’ Lacrosse

Grade-based programs run from kindergarten through eighth grade. Younger players focus on development; older age groups may have competitive and developmental brackets. Team availability varies by season.

Girls’ Lacrosse

Grade-based lacrosse from kindergarten through eighth grade. Confirm the next season’s team groups and availability.

Uniforms and Equipment

The program uses Parkland lacrosse uniforms. Ask about required uniform purchases, replacement pieces, and loan equipment before registering. Loan equipment is subject to availability and may require a refundable deposit.

Additional Training

SPYA has offered box lacrosse, skills sessions, and clinics. The older announcements do not establish a current schedule; contact the program about upcoming opportunities.
