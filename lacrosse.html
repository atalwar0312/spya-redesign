Room to grow.

Mini-Scoopers

A playful introduction listed for ages 3–5. The source describes a weekly seven-week program with sticks and soft balls supplied.

Boys' & girls' teams

Published grade groups are K–2, 3–4, 5–6 and 7–8. Team structure and competitive brackets vary.

Equipment & uniforms

Loaner equipment is described as first come, first served, with refundable deposits for boys and girls. Uniforms may require a separate purchase.

Volunteering

The published program includes a reimbursable family volunteer fee. Ask about eligible roles, hours and the reimbursement process.

Before you registerThe source mixes a 2026 season announcement with a 2024 Mini-Scoopers deadline. Confirm all upcoming dates, charges and equipment availability.
