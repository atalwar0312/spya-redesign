Program Overview

Fall Field Hockey

A recreational program in the Lehigh Valley Youth Field Hockey League. Mandatory evaluations help form balanced teams; they are not selection tryouts.

Spring Skills and Drills

A skills-focused season without games. Contact the commissioner for the next schedule and grade groups.

Program Philosophy

Learn the game, build skills, make friends, and compete in a safe, enjoyable environment. The program relies on volunteers.
